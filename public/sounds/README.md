# Chessed interaction sounds

`move.wav`, `capture.wav`, `check.wav`, and `checkmate.wav` are original,
procedurally synthesized Chessed assets. They were created for this repository
with `scripts/generate-chessed-sounds.mjs`; no sounds were copied, sampled, or
extracted from Chess.com, Lichess, or another chess application.

They are distributed under the repository's MIT license. The short decaying
tones share one oscillator family, with pitch, chord size, and duration used to
distinguish the four events. Re-run the generator from the repository root to
reproduce the committed WAV files byte-for-byte.
